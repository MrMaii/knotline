# Knotline Brand Direction

## Core

Knotline makes complex project relationships visible, connected, and actionable.

The product is a project operating map inside DeepSeek Harness. Its brand should feel like a calm control surface for work that is otherwise difficult to see: precise enough for engineers, clear enough for teams, and restrained enough to keep attention on the map.

## Voice

- Calm, precise, and direct.
- Technical without sounding cold or performative.
- Confident about structure; never loud about automation.
- Describe what is connected, what is moving, and what needs attention.

Avoid hype, anthropomorphic AI language, generic productivity promises, and visual noise that competes with the relationship graph.

## Visual system

- Primary ink: near-black / charcoal.
- Signal accent: electric indigo used for one active connection or decision point.
- Surfaces: white and quiet cool gray in light mode; near-black and deep slate in dark mode.
- Geometry: rounded nodes, clean paths, compact circular endpoints, generous whitespace.
- Logo mark: three connected endpoints crossed by a single indigo signal path. Use the mark without adding a generic map or chatbot symbol.

## Product application

The mark is used in the Map header and the DSH sidebar launcher. The DSH logo remains the host brand; Knotline is identified by its own mark and wordmark inside the plugin surface.
